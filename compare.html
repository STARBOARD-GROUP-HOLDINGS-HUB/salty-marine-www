Sodium-Ion vs Lithium vs AGM

No chemistry is right for every boat. Here's how the three options actually compare for marine house banks and stabilizer power — including where sodium-ion is not the answer.

Attribute Sodium-Ion Lithium (LiFePO₄) AGM (Lead-Acid)

Upfront cost Request a model-specific installed quote and warranty. Request a model-specific installed quote and warranty. Request a model-specific installed quote and warranty.

This is a checklist, not a ranking or a product specification. Compare actual manufacturer data under the same test conditions, then review the vessel's load profile and installation requirements before choosing a bank.

Consider sodium-ion when

  • You can review test data for a specific cell and pack.
  • Material sourcing or recycling is part of your purchasing criteria.
  • You need to evaluate a bank against measured daily loads.
  • The approved charging-temperature range matters to your use.

Consider LiFePO₄ when

  • Installed weight and usable capacity are binding constraints.
  • Your design includes the required battery management system.
  • Pack-level test data matches the proposed installation.

Consider AGM when

  • The bank is primarily for standby or starting duties.
  • Upfront cost is a binding constraint.
  • Compatibility with an existing system must be evaluated.
